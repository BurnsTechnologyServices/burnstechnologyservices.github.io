Approvals & operational tools

Make requests, decisions and outstanding work visible.

Replace scattered forms and email chains with a practical request, approval and status workflow.

Discuss this service↗

The problem we work on.

Requests move through email, documents and separate status spreadsheets. It is hard to see who needs to act, which details are missing and what has been approved.

A useful first project

Create one purchase-request form, an agreed approval route and a status view showing outstanding actions.

Who this is for

Operations, project and finance teams coordinating requests across people or departments.

Before we build

Access and inputs

Current forms, approval rules, roles, access requirements and examples of rejected or incomplete requests.

Scope to confirm

Authorised customer staff retain spending and payment approval. The workflow supports their decisions.

How will we check it?

Check approval permissions, missing documents, rejected requests, resubmissions and visibility of outstanding actions.

Where does it run?

We agree the environment, permissions, credentials and operating responsibilities before implementation. Existing customer tools are considered first.

What does it cost?

Any paid discovery or pilot is scoped and agreed before starting. Implementation is quoted against named deliverables. Software licences, hosting and usage charges are identified separately.

What happens after delivery?

The proposal covers documentation, handover, rights and licences. Monitoring, maintenance and change allowances can be agreed where useful.
