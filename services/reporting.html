Reporting & document automation

Make the recurring reporting process repeatable.

Prepare Excel reports, management packs and documents from agreed data sources, with visible checks and exceptions.

Discuss this service↗

The problem we work on.

People repeatedly download exports, reconcile spreadsheets and paste figures into the same report or presentation. The production steps take attention away from reviewing the result.

A useful first project

Reproduce a monthly management pack from agreed exports, check totals and periods, and flag missing inputs for review.

Who this is for

Finance teams, accountants, operations teams and organisations producing repeated reporting packs.

Before we build

Access and inputs

Sample source exports, a known reporting pack, expected totals and a customer reviewer who can confirm the business meaning.

Scope to confirm

Reporting production supports the customer reviewer. Accounting judgements and advice remain with the relevant professionals.

Testing, operation and cost

Agree what good looks like.

How will we check it?

Reproduce known reporting periods, reconcile totals and confirm the treatment of missing or restated data.

Where does it run?

We agree the environment, permissions, credentials and operating responsibilities before implementation. Existing customer tools are considered first.

What does it cost?

Any paid discovery or pilot is scoped and agreed before starting. Implementation is quoted against named deliverables. Software licences, hosting and usage charges are identified separately.

What happens after delivery?

The proposal covers documentation, handover, rights and licences. Monitoring, maintenance and change allowances can be agreed where useful.
