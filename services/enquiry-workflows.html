Enquiries & follow-up workflows

Give every enquiry an owner and a next step.

Collect the right details, assign work and keep quotations and follow-up tasks visible to your team.

Discuss this service↗

The problem we work on.

Requests arrive with missing details, ownership is unclear or open quotations are difficult to follow. Staff need one useful record and a clear next action.

A useful first project

Turn an incoming request into a structured enquiry, assign an owner and prepare follow-up tasks or reply drafts for staff approval.

Who this is for

Installers, field-service companies and service businesses handling recurring incoming requests.

Before we build

Access and inputs

Examples of historical enquiries, the existing inbox or CRM process, owner roles and agreed routing rules.

Scope to confirm

Customer staff retain responsibility for technical advice, prices and commitments. SMS, voice and additional platforms are scoped separately.

Describe your workflow↗
What you receive

Deliverables you can review.

01

A structured intake

Agreed required fields, attachments where appropriate and a clear enquiry record.

02

Ownership and routing

Rules that send each type of request to the right role or existing system.

03

Follow-up visibility

Tasks, reminders and an agreed view of open enquiries or quotations.

04

Approved reply workflows

Reusable drafts and review steps, with technical advice and prices approved by staff.

How will we check it?

Test complete and incomplete enquiries, ownership, duplicate requests, reminders and staff approval before external replies.

Where does it run?

We agree the environment, permissions, credentials and operating responsibilities before implementation. Existing customer tools are considered first.

What does it cost?

Any paid discovery or pilot is scoped and agreed before starting. Implementation is quoted against named deliverables. Software licences, hosting and usage charges are identified separately.

What happens after delivery?

The proposal covers documentation, handover, rights and licences. Monitoring, maintenance and change allowances can be agreed where useful.
