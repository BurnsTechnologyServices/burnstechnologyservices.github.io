Practical AI assistants

Give AI a useful job with clear boundaries.

Set up assistants for approved-source research, internal information and draft preparation, with human review built in.

Discuss this service↗

The problem we work on.

Staff have access to AI tools but lack shared instructions, reliable source material and a repeatable way to review the output. A useful assistant needs a defined job and realistic tests.

A useful first project

An internal assistant that searches approved support material, identifies relevant sources and prepares a reply draft for staff to check.

Who this is for

Small teams with a specific research, internal knowledge or administrative task.

Before we build

Access and inputs

Approved source documents, representative tasks, the client AI environment and a named reviewer.

Scope to confirm

The assistant can make mistakes. Final business decisions and external commitments remain with people. Client subscriptions and usage are identified separately.

Describe your workflow↗
What you receive

Deliverables you can review.

01

A defined assistant role

The permitted tasks, users, tools and situations that require human review.

02

Organised approved sources

An agreed set of information with source and access responsibilities.

03

Tested instructions

Representative tasks, uncertain cases and checks against source material.

04

A practical handover

How staff use, review and maintain the assistant and its source information.

A proposed assistant workflow

Find. Draft. Review.

A member of staff asks a question. The assistant checks approved documents, identifies sources and prepares a draft. A person reviews the answer before using it externally.

Example output structure:
Suggested response
Source document and relevant section
Information missing or uncertain
Action required from the reviewer

This is an illustrative workflow, not a live AI chatbot.
Testing, operation and cost

Agree what good looks like.

How will we check it?

Test source attribution, missing information, uncertain questions and review responsibilities before wider use.

Where does it run?

We agree the environment, permissions, credentials and operating responsibilities before implementation. Existing customer tools are considered first.

What does it cost?

Any paid discovery or pilot is scoped and agreed before starting. Implementation is quoted against named deliverables. Software licences, hosting and usage charges are identified separately.

What happens after delivery?

The proposal covers documentation, handover, rights and licences. Monitoring, maintenance and change allowances can be agreed where useful.
