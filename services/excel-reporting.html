Excel & Reporting

Tidy spreadsheets, check your data and make recurring reports easier to produce.

Ask about this service↗

Spend less time rebuilding the same spreadsheet or report.

If your team repeatedly cleans exports, fixes formulas or copies figures into reports, there may be a simpler way to do the job. We work from an agreed example and the result your team needs.

Does this sound familiar?

  • This spreadsheet takes too long to update.
  • Supplier files arrive in different formats and need checking.
  • We rebuild the same report or presentation every month.

Who this is for: Owners, administrators, operations teams and finance teams working with repeated files or reporting packs.

A useful first step

Bring an authorised sample input and an example of the finished spreadsheet or report. We’ll identify one useful improvement to build and check.
