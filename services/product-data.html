Product & supplier data

Turn inconsistent files into a checked catalogue.

Clean, match and reshape supplier files for your existing product system, with uncertain changes kept visible.

Discuss this service↗

The problem we work on.

Each supplier sends a different format. Staff clean columns, match codes and check prices or pack sizes before importing the data. One unchecked change can travel into the product catalogue.

A useful first project

Standardise an agreed supplier feed, preserve product identifiers and flag missing codes, invalid prices and uncertain matches before import.

Who this is for

Distributors, wholesalers, retailers and teams maintaining recurring supplier catalogues.

Before we build

Access and inputs

Authorised supplier samples, destination import specifications and confirmed rules for product codes, prices, tax and units.

Scope to confirm

Supplier formats and destination requirements are agreed individually. Live stock, extra suppliers and additional destinations need their own scope.

How will we check it?

Reconcile input and output counts, preserve codes, test pack sizes and flag missing, duplicate or uncertain records.

Where does it run?

We agree the environment, permissions, credentials and operating responsibilities before implementation. Existing customer tools are considered first.

What does it cost?

Any paid discovery or pilot is scoped and agreed before starting. Implementation is quoted against named deliverables. Software licences, hosting and usage charges are identified separately.

What happens after delivery?

The proposal covers documentation, handover, rights and licences. Monitoring, maintenance and change allowances can be agreed where useful.
