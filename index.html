Do we need to replace our existing software?

We first look at what your existing systems can already do. A connection, configuration change or focused tool may solve the problem. Access to an API or suitable export needs to be checked before we promise an integration.

Is this only for businesses that already use AI?

No. Many useful improvements use ordinary software, data processing or workflow rules. AI is useful for some tasks, such as preparing drafts or finding information, and we use it where the task and review process make sense.

What does a project cost?

Cost depends on the systems, data, scope and support needed. We agree any paid discovery or pilot before starting, then prepare a project quote. Software subscriptions, hosting and usage charges are identified separately where relevant.

What should I bring to an initial conversation?

Describe one task your team repeats, the software involved and what you would like to improve. A rough idea of frequency and time spent helps. Please avoid sending passwords, sensitive records or customer data in an initial enquiry.

Can you support the workflow after handover?

Ongoing monitoring, maintenance or small changes can be included where useful. Support hours, response expectations and change allowances are agreed for each engagement.
