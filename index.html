06 / Before we talk

Useful answers
to practical questions.

Clear expectations help you decide whether a project is worth exploring.

Can you work with our existing software?

We first check the features, API, database access or exports your existing systems provide. The proposed connection and its limits are confirmed during scoping.

What does a project cost?

The fee depends on scope, access, data and the support needed. Any paid discovery or pilot is agreed first, followed by an implementation quote. Client software, hosting and usage charges are identified separately.

Where will the solution run?

That depends on the tools and the customer environment. Hosting, access controls, credentials and responsibility for operation are agreed in the project scope.

Who owns and maintains the work?

The proposal sets out software and source-code rights, third-party licences, documentation, handover and maintenance responsibilities. These are agreed before implementation.

Will we need ongoing support?

Some tools can be handed over for your team to run. Others benefit from monitoring or maintenance. Support hours, response expectations and change allowances are agreed individually.

How should we prepare for a first conversation?

Describe one recurring process, the systems involved and where the effort goes. Bring a rough idea of frequency and volume. Start with a description; authorised sample data can be arranged later.

Start with the process you want to improve.

Tell us what happens today, which systems you use and where the work gets stuck.
