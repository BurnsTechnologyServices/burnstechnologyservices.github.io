Website privacy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This notice describes this website and initial enquiries to Burns Technology Services, Sligo, Ireland. For questions about your information, email ryandavidburns@hotmail.com.

Visiting the website

The website does not set analytics or advertising cookies, load third-party tracking scripts, or use visitor accounts. Fonts and illustrations are part of the website, rather than loaded from external font or image services.

If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes technical information associated with website visits. GitHub states that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

The enquiry form

The form runs in your browser and prepares a message in your email application. It does not send a request to a form service, store entries in this website, or automatically send an email. You decide whether to send the prepared message.

When you email us

If you send an enquiry, Ryan receives the details you choose to include, such as your name, email address, business, systems and project description. These are used to understand and respond to your enquiry and discuss the work you requested. Your email provider and the receiving email provider process the email as part of delivery.

Enquiries are handled as needed for the discussion and any resulting work. Please contact Ryan if you want to ask about access, correction or deletion of information you have provided. Applicable record-keeping obligations may affect what can be deleted. You may also raise a concern with the Irish Data Protection Commission.

Project information

Arrangements for access to systems, customer data, confidentiality and project-specific processing are agreed separately before work requires them.

Please keep passwords, access tokens and sensitive personal or customer records out of an initial enquiry. Start with a description of the process you want to improve.
