Website privacy.

How this website, its demonstrations and initial project enquiries handle information.

Updated 26 October 2026

Who to contact

Burns Technology Services is based in Sligo, Ireland. For enquiries about this website or information you have provided, contact Ryan Burns at burnstechnologyservices@gmail.com.

Visiting the website

The website does not set analytics or advertising cookies, use visitor accounts or load external font services. GitHub Pages hosts the public website. GitHub processes technical visit information, including IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its privacy statement.

The working demonstrations

The catalogue, enquiry and reporting examples run in your browser tab. They do not send their inputs to Burns Technology Services, Web3Forms or an AI provider. Files selected for the catalogue demonstration are read locally in your browser. Downloads are generated in your browser. The demonstrations do not use persistent browser storage; changes reset when you reload.

Please use sample data in the demonstrations. They are examples of software behaviour, rather than a production service for processing your business records.

Sending a project enquiry

The contact form sends your name, email address, optional business name, service selection, message and an enquiry reference to Web3Forms for email delivery to Burns Technology Services. Your email address is used as the reply-to address. Web3Forms processes technical information for delivery and spam prevention. Its policy describes processing through infrastructure and spam-filtering providers, including transfers outside the European Economic Area. See Web3Forms’ privacy policy and data processing agreement.

Web3Forms states that submission data is retained for up to three years unless a shorter period applies or it is deleted earlier. Email copies are also handled by the receiving email provider. Contact Ryan about information you have submitted, or email him directly instead of using the form.

Your details remain in the current page during submission. A timeout means delivery is unconfirmed; a delayed request may still arrive. References help identify follow-ups. Details are not saved in browser storage and are lost when you close or reload the page. The copy and email-draft options prepare your message locally; an email draft is sent only when you press Send in your own email app.

How enquiry information is used

Ryan uses your information to respond, understand the work you requested and discuss a possible engagement. Depending on the enquiry, this supports steps you request before an agreement or the legitimate interest in responding to a business enquiry. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing.

Enquiry records are kept as needed for the discussion, any resulting work and applicable record-keeping obligations. Contact Ryan if you want to ask about access, correction, deletion or an objection to processing. The circumstances and applicable obligations affect which rights apply. You can also raise a concern with the Irish Data Protection Commission.

Project data and access

System access, customer data, confidentiality, hosting and any project-specific processing arrangements are agreed separately before work requires them.

Keep passwords, access tokens and sensitive personal or customer records out of an initial enquiry. Start with a description of the task.
